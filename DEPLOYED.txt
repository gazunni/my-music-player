TITLE:
Generify Presents: Emotional Lenses

STATUS:
Live at music.generify.ca. Fixed the real cause of "drag not working" on iOS landscape: the visible landscape progress bar (.np-progress-panel) never had any seek listeners attached — only the hidden portrait bar did. Touches on it (or its 3px-thin track) fell through to the swipe-to-skip handler, which restarts the track from 0 when you drag rightward more than 3s in. Both bars now share the same drag logic and the panel bar has a generous invisible hit area. See CHANGELOG.md for version history.
